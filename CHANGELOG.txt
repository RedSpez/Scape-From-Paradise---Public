#Version | v0.0.1.0 | 9/03/2026 | The movement update

Here it is the first big update of the pre-alpha state. I was starting to get a little stagnant with the development of this version but I suddenly got a lot of inspiration and mental 
clarity that allowed me to fix a bunch of problems I was having to implementent the stamina system properly and now it's finally done. But not only that, I managed to fix a bunch more of other issues
and integrate a few mechanics that I've forgot to add before. I think I'm finally starting to understand the GASP system and with that a lot of important things will be faster and easier to do for me
in the future. I really like the progress of this version but still a long road before finishing the pre-alpha state. Hopefully, this stage will not take me more than tree months. 

*Fixed the bug that let you consume all your stamina when pressing space bar repeatedly.

*Fixed infinite stamina bug. Now stamina work as intended when sprinting or aiming. 

*Stamina is no longer depleted when pressing the space bar during any climbing action.

*Climbing now depletes the arms stamina.

*Reduced arms stamina consumption when aiming.

*Reduced arms stamina recovery.

*Significantly reduced stamina consumption when sprinting.

*Reduced stamina recovery when running.

*Increased stamina recovery when walking or standing still.

*Now there's a slight delay to recover stamina after any amount of depletion. 

*Modified movement, aiming and shooting behavior:

-Removed zoom when aiming. Zoom function has been transfered to a different keybind.

-Now you can only shoot when aiming.

-Now you can't aim while you're sprinting.

-Now you can't start to sprint while you're aiming.

*Zoom in function transfered to "V" keybind.  

*Moved flashlight attach point to the head instead of the camera.

*Fixed aiming offset. Now bullets finally go to where you aim.

*Added free directional movement when sprinting. Now you can change your movement direction quickly without moving the camera. 

*Fixed a bug that caused to damage yourself when shooting while aiming to the ground.

*Modified main menu layout added sounds when hovering and clicking on buttons.

*Replaced level selection menu. Now it shows a map where you can choose between the different locations that will be avaliable in the future. The map was made using Inkarnate and the effects were
added with AI. The image is a placeholder.

*Removed GASP level.

*Reduced aiming dot size.
